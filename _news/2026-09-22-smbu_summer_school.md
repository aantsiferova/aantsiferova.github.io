Co-organized the two-week summer school **"Multimodal Intelligence and AI Security"** at [Shenzhen MSU-BIT University (SMBU)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenzhen_MSU-BIT_University), China! 🇨🇳 The program featured lectures by Alex Kot, Kede Ma, and Wenhan Yang, followed by 9 research challenges solved by 10 mixed Russian-Chinese student teams. The team I mentored won with an adversarial patch that breaks the verification system of a smart camera! 🏆
