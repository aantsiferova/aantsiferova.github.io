Digital attacks assume full access to the input image, but real systems see the world through a camera. We proposed Ti-patch, a tiled physical adversarial patch against no-reference video quality metrics {% cite vatolinTipatch2024 %}, and demonstrated physical-digital patch attacks on real-world cameras at [PerCom 2026](https://percom.org/accepted-papers-demo/).
