After showing that modern quality metrics are easy to manipulate, we studied how to protect them. We investigated whether adversarial purification methods from image classification transfer to quality assessment and proposed new purification methods {% cite vatolinAdversarial2024 %}, and studied adversarial training for image quality assessment models {% cite turdakovIncreasing2024 %}.

The results were summarized in the first benchmark of defenses for image quality metrics, systematically evaluating 25 defense strategies — adversarial purification, adversarial training and certified robustness methods — against a wide range of attacks {% cite antsiferovaGuardians2024 %}.
