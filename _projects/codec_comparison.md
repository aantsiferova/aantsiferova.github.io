The MSU Video Codecs Comparison is a long-running series of independent evaluations of video encoders {% cite antsiferovaMsu2019 %}, extended to cloud transcoding services {% cite antsiferovaVideo2019 %}.

To make such comparisons reliable, we developed BSQ-rate, a new approach to video codec performance comparison that addresses drawbacks of BD-rate {% cite vatolinBsqrate2020 %}, studied which objective quality metrics best predict subjective quality in codec comparisons {% cite vatolinApplying2021 %}, and built a shot boundary detection method with a new extensive dataset {% cite vatolinShot2021 %}.
