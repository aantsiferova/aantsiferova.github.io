Learned image codecs, including the upcoming JPEG AI standard, may be manipulated by small input perturbations that cause severe artifacts or bitrate growth. We proposed a methodology for evaluating the adversarial robustness of JPEG AI and other neural codecs {% cite antsiferovaExploring2024 %}, released NIC-RobustBench, an open-source toolkit for neural image compression robustness analysis {% cite antsiferovaNicrobustbench2025 %}, and studied modular adversarial optimization targeting both global distortion and local artifacts {% cite vatolinTreating %}.
