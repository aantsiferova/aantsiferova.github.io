**Role:** Project Head | **Period:** Mar 2021 -- Jun 2025

Developed the biggest benchmark of video-compression-related quality metrics. The benchmark received positive feedback from industry leaders, such as Huawei, Tencent, Yandex, Google (YouTube Media Algorithms), raised funding and received new methods submissions from companies. The dataset is published online, having an average of 15 downloads per month by leading industry companies and research institutes.
