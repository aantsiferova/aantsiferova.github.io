**Role:** Project Head | **Period:** Mar 2021 -- Jun 2025

Developed the biggest benchmark of video-compression-related quality metrics {% cite kulikovVideo2022 %}. The benchmark received positive feedback from industry leaders, such as Huawei, Tencent, Yandex, Google (YouTube Media Algorithms), raised funding and received new methods submissions from companies. The dataset is published online, having an average of 15 downloads per month by leading industry companies and research institutes.

The work was continued with the LEHA-CVQAD dataset for generalized quality assessment of compression artifacts {% cite antsiferovaLehacvqad2025 %} and the AIM 2024 challenge on compressed video quality assessment {% cite tuAim2024 %}. Earlier, we studied the limits of no-reference metrics for compressed video {% cite vatolinBarriers2019 %} and proposed the ERQA metric for video super-resolution {% cite vatolinErqa2021 %}.
